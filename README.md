> You either die a hero, or you live long enough to see yourself become the villain.

> It's a feature, not a bug.

I was sipping on my OJ with my trusted friend and fellow digital nomad Joe S when I realised he committed and merged his branch with the `main` branch, effectively erasing months of my work. I was furious.

## 2. Tragedy ([McDonald's, Bohaterów Monte Cassino St](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Restauracja+McDonald's/@54.4433347,18.5600409,17z/data=!3m2!4b1!5s0x46fda386d1713f35:0xd59df7ce6db34870!4m6!3m5!1s0x46fd0a9046757e13:0x1f02168bf6c8ffd7!8m2!3d54.4433347!4d18.5626158!16s%2Fg%2F1tj518_t?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAwNy4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), Sopot, Poland) 
This time my friend Beni M and me were located on the patio of a cozy McDonald's on Monte-Cassino St. in Poland, when I again had to witness months of my hard work being erased in a merger between branches. This was the straw that broke the camel's back. I vowed for vengeance and I begun my James Bond Villain arc.
